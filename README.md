# Wall Smacker

Smack the wall

## Play

- Click or tap **Punch Wall** to deal damage. Space also punches when focus is on the page or the punch button.
- At 25 manual punches, **Punch Harder** unlocks at level 1. Its first paid level costs 30 TP. At Lv. 5 it reveals **Plant Your Feet** and **Punching Drill**.
- Early generators unlock after one level in the previous generator. Later generators require Lv. 5 or Lv. 10 in the previous generator. Action-specific generators have their own prerequisites. Prices rise with prerequisite depth, with steeper level costs after Lv. 6 and a 50% price multiplier from Lv. 10 for generators. Lv. 10 doubles how often a generator runs, or doubles Counter Rhythm’s follow-up damage.
- Skills unlock when you own the levels listed in their prerequisites. Skill starting prices are shared across branches and rise with the depth of the prerequisite chain. Each purchased level raises that item’s next price. For example, Punching Drill Lv. 5 reveals Quick Hands and Plant Your Feet Lv. 3 reveals Find Your Rhythm. Purchases and TP balances carry over from earlier saves.
- Click or tap the colored weak spot directly on the wall before its timer expires, or land fast and heavy hits to fill a spreading crack. The wall changes material as you breach layers.

Progress autosaves in the browser. In **Options**, export a JSON backup and import it on another device or after moving the project. Automatic generators operate while the game is open. Temporary combat effects reset when you reload or change walls.
